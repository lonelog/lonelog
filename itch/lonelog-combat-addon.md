**Combat is where solo play gets dense.** Dice fly, hit points drop, positions shift, and you're running both sides. Without structure, your log turns into a wall of text where you can't tell who hit whom or when things went wrong.

The **Combat Add-on** gives you just enough structure to keep fights readable, without turning your session into a spreadsheet. It layers on top of core Lonelog and uses the same `@`, `d:`, `->` and `=>` symbols.

## What it adds

- **Combatant tags** `[F:Thug|HP 6|pos:Close]` track each foe's stats and status.
- **Combat blocks** `[COMBAT]` … `[/COMBAT]` mark where an encounter starts and ends.
- **Round markers** `Rd1`, `Rd2`, `Rd3` separate initiative rounds.
- **Actor attribution** `@(Thug A) Lunges at me` shows who acts when it isn't you.
- **Round rosters** snapshot every combatant's state at a round boundary.

No new core symbols. Use as much or as little as the fight needs: a quick skirmish takes a line or two, a five-round battle with six combatants stays easy to follow.

## When you need it

If your system settles a fight with a single roll, core Lonelog already covers it. This add-on is for games where you track rounds, multiple combatants, ranges and changing hit points.

## Requires

[Lonelog](https://lonelog.itch.io/lonelog), the core notation. Like every add-on, it's optional: use it when your game needs it.
