**"Q of spades", "queen♠", "QS".** Without a standard, card draws turn into a mess of spellings, and tarot is worse: "reversed Tower", "The Tower (rev.)" and "XVI r" are all the same card.

The **Card Notation Add-on** gives every card a short, unambiguous name, so draws read the same in every log.

## What it covers

- **Standard playing cards**, the 52-card deck plus jokers: `tbl: draw=Qs -> Dark omen`
- **Tarot**: Major Arcana by number, Minor Arcana by rank and suit, and reversed cards: `tbl: draw=M16r`
- **Oracle decks**: a light convention for named-card systems
- **Spreads** with named positions: `tbl: Past=5s, Present=Ks`
- **Deck state and hands**: what's left in the deck and what you're holding

No new core symbols. Cards follow the same pattern as dice: `=` is the card you drew, `->` is what it means, `=>` is what happens next.

## When you need it

If you play one system with one deck and never share logs, your current spelling probably works. Adopt this when consistency matters: shared logs, tool support, or switching between card systems.

## Requires

[Lonelog](https://lonelog.itch.io/lonelog), the core notation. Like every add-on, it's optional: use it when your game needs it.
