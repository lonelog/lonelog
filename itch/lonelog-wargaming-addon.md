**Twelve soldiers, morale cracking under fire.** Solo wargaming tracks units, not heroes, across turns that last minutes and campaigns that last months.

The **Solo Wargaming Add-on** brings Lonelog to that scale: unit tags, battle structure, turn markers, and a campaign block that carries your warband from one battle to the next.

## What it adds

- **Unit tags** `[Unit:Ironclad Rifles|×12|Morale 8|status:Fresh]` track model count, morale and status.
- **Force tags** record a side's leader, size and goal.
- **Battle blocks** `[BATTLE]` … `[/BATTLE]` and **turn markers** `Tn1`, `Tn2` structure the fight.
- **Hit-location armor** for mechs and vehicles: `Armor CT22/RT18/LT18`
- **Campaign blocks** `[CAMPAIGN]` … `[/CAMPAIGN]` record casualties, advances and spoils between sessions.

Units activate with `@(Unit Name)`, the actor convention you already know. No new core symbols.

## When you need it

A fight between a few named characters is a job for the [Combat Add-on](https://lonelog.itch.io/lonelog-combat-addon). Reach for this one when the unit, not the individual, is what you're moving on the table.

## Requires

[Lonelog](https://lonelog.itch.io/lonelog), the core notation. Like every add-on, it's optional: use it when your game needs it.
